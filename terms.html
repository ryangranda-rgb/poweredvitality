1. Who this is with

You're hiring Ryan Granda (Granda Strength / Powered Vitality LLC) for online fitness coaching — a personalized training plan, check-ins, and habit support built around a real work week.

Ryan is a NASM-CPT and Natural pro bodybuilder (PNBA). This is fitness coaching, not medical, clinical, or chiropractic care. If something needs a licensed clinician, we'll pause that piece of the plan and you'll take it to a qualified professional.

2. What's included

For the length of your enrollment, you get:

  • A personalized training plan in Everfit
  • Weekly check-ins in Everfit chat
  • One monthly coaching call (about 30 minutes)
  • Nutrition and recovery guidance within coaching scope (not medical nutrition therapy)
  • Replies Monday–Saturday on the agreed channel — helpful and timely, but not an instant-reply promise
  • Plan adjustments as we learn what fits your week

Not included: medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, PEDs, or a promise of a specific body result (weight, look, or physique). Progress depends on your consistency, sleep, stress, and the plan we build together.

Sunday is a rest day for coaching replies.

5. 30-day conditional guarantee (fit policy)

This is a fit policy, not a promise of a body result. Individual results vary. Natural training only.

Who it covers

Paid clients on the founding or standard rates, or any later paid rate Ryan sets for those paths.
Not covered: complimentary pilots · any other exception Ryan puts in writing.

Clock

Day 1 = coaching start = Everfit access is live and your approved plan is assigned.
Claim window: day 30 through day 37 (you ask once, in writing).
Late claims: Claims after day 37 are declined. Ryan may waive the deadline in writing on a case-by-case basis.

To be eligible

  1. Complete every check-in, and
  2. Do at least 80% of assigned workouts (the optional Lift D doesn't count), and
  3. Follow at least 80% of nutrition days over days 1–30.

Workouts and nutrition each have to reach 80% on their own.

Nutrition day = followed when: logged in Everfit by the next morning, protein target hit, and calories within ±10% of target.
Unlogged day = not followed. Day Ryan approved as off-plan = followed.

If eligible and still not a fit, the client chooses a refund or +30 days free.

Fitness Coach prepares the adherence report. Ryan makes the final eligibility call.

How to claim

Ask by iMessage or email between day 30 and day 37. One claim per enrollment.

If Ryan confirms you're eligible, you choose one:

(A) Refund

  • Monthly: your latest successful coaching payment back.
  • Pay-in-full: exactly one-third of what you paid. The exact amount is stated at your signup.

Once a refund is claimed, unused prepaid coaching ends. Access runs to the end of the current paid month (pay-in-full: the current 4-week period). Approved refunds go out within 7 days.

(B) +30 days of coaching free

  • Extra coaching at no charge while we fix what's not working.
  • Not an automatic renewal and not a free-to-paid conversion by itself. When the free window ends, normal end/cancel rules apply. Monthly billing is paused/pushed for that window by Ryan/Finance; pay-in-full access is extended past the original block end. If monthly billing would resume after Option B, you keep the same cancel rights as §7.

You pick A or B once. Choosing one uses the guarantee for that enrollment.
