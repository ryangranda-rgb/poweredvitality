Application and review

The application is for adults who are 18 or older. Provide contact details where Ryan can reach you and use the bounded choices to describe your nonmedical goals, schedule and support needs. Do not submit diagnoses, injuries, medications, health records, photos or other sensitive health information.

Ryan reviews fit and availability personally. Applying does not guarantee acceptance. If appropriate, Ryan offers times for a 30-minute Zoom consultation by email, confirms the chosen time and time zone, and sends the Zoom invitation. An invitation or link visit does not confirm attendance or enrollment.

Coaching and the disclosed offer

Granda Strength's proposed coaching is entirely online exercise planning, general nutrition recommendations and lifestyle habits. It is not in-person training or medical diagnosis or treatment.

The Working Man's 12-Week Strength Blueprint: the first 3 paying clients may choose $199/month, kept as long as they stay enrolled, or $570 paid in full for 12 weeks. After the first 3 paying clients, the rate is $297/month, or $850 paid in full for 12 weeks. Monthly coaching has no minimum commitment. You can cancel any time before the next charge, with no notice period. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current paid period. You can cancel online through the Stripe customer portal. Your portal link is provided at enrollment. You can also cancel by email or iMessage to Ryan. Founding clients keep $199/month as long as they stay enrolled; if you cancel and rejoin later, the current rate applies. After a 12-week pay-in-full block, there is no automatic renewal. You can opt in to continue month to month at your rate. Statutory rights are preserved.

Monthly plan: $199/month (founding; $297/month after the first 3 clients), billed monthly and continuing until you cancel. Cancel anytime before your next charge online through the Stripe customer portal, or by email or iMessage; cancellation takes effect at the end of your paid period.

30-day guarantee: Complete every check-in and follow at least 80% of your assigned workouts and 80% of your nutrition days. A nutrition day counts when it's logged, protein is hit, and calories are within 10% of target. If it's still not a fit, you choose either a refund or 30 extra days of coaching free. Monthly clients are refunded their latest payment. Pay-in-full clients are refunded exactly one-third of what they paid ($190 on the $570 plan; $283.33 on the $850 plan). If you choose the refund, coaching access ends the day the refund is issued. Claim by iMessage or email between day 30 and day 37. Claims after day 37 are declined unless Ryan waives the deadline. I review each claim personally. Approved refunds go out within 7 days.

The $199/month founding option starts with the application. Ryan confirms fit and rate before that enrollment. A separate finalized client agreement and recurring-payment consent process are required before monthly paid coaching. Agreement signing and private health intake are not completed through the public application. Monthly checkout starts billing immediately on an agreed plan-start date. Applying itself creates no charge. The $570 founding pay-in-full price is listed on the site. Pay-in-full link is sent after your fit consultation.
