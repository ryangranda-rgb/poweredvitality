Website and application terms

Updated October 7, 2026

These terms describe the public website and coaching application. Merely viewing the website or submitting an application does not enroll you in paid coaching, sign an agreement, authorize payment, accept a health or exercise-risk waiver, represent medical readiness, or consent to marketing or SMS.

Application and review

The application is for adults who are 18 or older. Provide contact details where Ryan can reach you and use the bounded choices to describe your nonmedical goals, schedule and support needs. Do not submit diagnoses, injuries, medications, health records, photos or other sensitive health information.

Ryan reviews fit and availability personally. Applying does not guarantee acceptance. If appropriate, Ryan offers times for a 30-minute Zoom consultation by email, confirms the chosen time and time zone, and sends the Zoom invitation. An invitation or link visit does not confirm attendance or enrollment.

Coaching and the disclosed offer

Powered Vitality's proposed coaching is entirely online exercise planning, general nutrition recommendations and lifestyle habits. It is not in-person training or medical diagnosis or treatment.

The founding offer is $199/month while continuously enrolled, with no minimum commitment. Canceling before the next renewal prevents that charge, with coaching through the paid period. After a subscription ends, rejoining uses the current disclosed price. Statutory rights are preserved.

A separate finalized client agreement and recurring-payment consent process are required before paid coaching. Agreement signing, payment enrollment and private health intake are currently unavailable through this website. Payment would occur on an agreed plan-start date because checkout starts billing immediately. Applying itself creates no charge.
