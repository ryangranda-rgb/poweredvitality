Coaching scope

Updated October 7, 2026

Powered Vitality describes entirely online exercise planning, general nutrition recommendations and lifestyle habits. The website and application do not provide medical diagnosis or treatment or in-person training.

Ryan is a natural pro bodybuilder and chiropractic student. The application does not establish independently licensed chiropractic care, assess medical readiness or collect a health-history intake.

Viewing the website or applying does not enroll you, sign a coaching agreement, authorize payment, waive claims, assume an exercise-risk agreement or consent to marketing or SMS. A separate finalized coaching agreement and approved private readiness process are required before coaching begins.

Do not submit diagnoses, injury details, medications, documents or photos through the public application. For application questions, email ryangrandafit@gmail.com.
