Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: May 29, 2026

At Powered Vitality LLC, we are committed to providing a high-quality, personalized coaching experience. We encourage you to read this Refund and Cancellation Policy carefully before enrolling in any of our Services. By enrolling, you acknowledge that you have read and agree to this Policy.

1. Nature of Services

Powered Vitality LLC provides personalized online fitness and nutrition coaching Services. Upon enrollment, we immediately begin the process of building a custom training program and nutrition plan specifically tailored to your individual goals, fitness history, schedule, equipment, health history, and stated limitations. Because each plan requires a significant and individualized investment of time and expertise, our refund policy reflects the nature of these custom deliverables.

2. Refund Eligibility

2.1 Seven-Day Pre-Delivery Refund Window

You are eligible for a full refund of your initial subscription payment if all of the following conditions are met:

  • Your refund request is submitted within seven (7) calendar days of your original purchase date; and
  • Your custom coaching plan — including your training program and nutrition plan — has not yet been delivered to you at the time of your request.

To request a refund within this window, contact us at ryangrandafit@gmail.com with your full name, the email address used at enrollment, and the date of your purchase. Eligible refunds will be processed within five (5) to ten (10) business days to the original payment method.

2.2 No Refund After Plan Delivery

Once your custom training program and nutrition plan have been delivered to you — which typically occurs within forty-eight (48) hours of the successful completion and clearance of your intake form and PAR-Q — your payment for that billing period is non-refundable. This policy applies regardless of:

  • Whether you have begun using the program;
  • Whether you are satisfied with the program;
  • Whether you have achieved specific results;
  • Whether you have completed your weekly check-ins;
  • Whether you decide to discontinue participation after delivery.

This policy exists because each custom plan represents a substantial, individualized investment of time, expertise, and resources that cannot be recovered once the deliverable has been provided to you. If you are experiencing dissatisfaction with your plan or challenges with the program, we strongly encourage you to contact us so that we may work with you to address your concerns, adjust your program, or find an appropriate solution.

2.3 Monthly Renewal Payments

Monthly subscription renewal payments are non-refundable. If you do not wish to be charged for a renewal cycle, you must cancel your subscription in accordance with Section 3 of this Policy prior to your renewal date. Powered Vitality LLC is not responsible for renewal charges that occur because a cancellation request was not submitted before the renewal date.

2.4 Delayed Plan Delivery

In the unlikely event that your custom plan is not delivered within forty-eight (48) hours of completing a fully cleared intake form, and this delay is solely attributable to Powered Vitality LLC with no incomplete information on your part, you may contact us at ryangrandafit@gmail.com to discuss your options, which may include an account credit or extension of your subscription term. Delays caused by incomplete, unclear, or uncleared intake submissions are not the responsibility of Powered Vitality LLC.

3. Cancellation Policy

3.1 How to Cancel

You may cancel your monthly subscription at any time by contacting us via email at ryangrandafit@gmail.com. To process your cancellation, please include the following information in your request:

  • Your full legal name;
  • The email address associated with your account;
  • Your request to cancel and the desired effective date (if other than end of current billing cycle).

Cancellation requests will be acknowledged and processed within two (2) business days of receipt. We recommend retaining a copy of your cancellation request for your records.

3.2 Effective Date of Cancellation

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, all cancellations take effect at the end of your current billing cycle. You will retain full access to your coaching program, Trainerize platform, and Powered Vitality community (Skool) through the end of your paid subscription period. Access to the Services will be deactivated at the conclusion of the final paid billing period.

3.3 No Prorated Refunds for Partial Months

Powered Vitality LLC does not provide prorated refunds for any unused days remaining in a billing cycle at the time of cancellation. Your subscription provides access to the Services for the full billing month, and you retain that access through the end of the paid period regardless of when your cancellation is submitted.

3.4 Reactivation After Cancellation

If you wish to re-enroll in the Services after canceling, you may do so by visiting https://poweredvitality.netlify.app and selecting a subscription tier. Re-enrollment is subject to the availability of coaching spots. Prior cancellation does not guarantee re-enrollment at previously held pricing.

4. Subscription Pauses

If you are experiencing circumstances that make it difficult to participate in the program — such as illness, injury, travel, or significant life events — we encourage you to contact us at ryangrandafit@gmail.com to discuss a temporary subscription pause rather than canceling entirely. Pauses are offered at the discretion of Powered Vitality LLC and are not guaranteed. Approved pauses will hold your spot and your program for a defined period, after which your subscription will resume at your current rate.

5. Failed Payments and Account Access

If a scheduled subscription payment fails for any reason, you will be notified promptly via email and/or SMS with a link to update your billing information. Your access to the Services will be maintained for a period of forty-eight (48) hours from the time of the failed payment notification to allow you to resolve the issue.

If payment is not received and your billing information is not updated within forty-eight (48) hours, your subscription will lapse and your access to the Services — including your coaching program, Trainerize platform, and community access — will be paused until the outstanding payment is successfully processed. Powered Vitality LLC is not responsible for any interruption in your program resulting from a failed payment.

Powered Vitality LLC reserves the right to permanently terminate accounts with repeated or unresolved payment failures.

6. Chargebacks and Payment Disputes

If you have a concern about a charge from Powered Vitality LLC, we strongly encourage you to contact us directly at ryangrandafit@gmail.com before initiating a chargeback or payment dispute with your bank, credit card issuer, or payment provider. The vast majority of billing concerns can be resolved quickly and amicably through direct communication, and we are committed to working with you in good faith.

Initiating a chargeback or payment dispute without first contacting Powered Vitality LLC may result in:

  • Immediate suspension or permanent termination of your account and access to all Services;
  • Forfeiture of any applicable refund eligibility;
  • Referral of the matter to a collections agency or legal counsel to recover amounts owed, including associated fees and costs;
  • Being flagged as ineligible for future enrollment.

Please note that your signed client service agreement, completed intake form, and plan delivery records constitute documented evidence that will be submitted in response to any chargeback filed with your payment provider.

7. Exceptions and Special Circumstances

Powered Vitality LLC recognizes that extraordinary circumstances may arise. Refund or cancellation requests arising from documented medical emergencies, serious illness, or other exceptional circumstances will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis at the sole discretion of Powered Vitality LLC. To request consideration under this provision, contact us at ryangrandafit@gmail.com with a written explanation and any relevant supporting documentation. Powered Vitality LLC is not obligated to grant exceptions but will make reasonable efforts to find an equitable solution.

8. Modifications to This Policy

Powered Vitality LLC reserves the right to modify this Refund and Cancellation Policy at any time. Changes will be communicated via email to active subscribers and reflected in the updated "Last updated" date at the top of this document. Your continued use of the Services after the effective date of any modification constitutes your acceptance of the updated Policy. Modifications will not apply retroactively to subscriptions enrolled prior to the effective date of the change.
