Payment and cancellation information

Updated October 30, 2026

No payment or paid enrollment can be completed through the public application. The final paid-coaching agreement and recurring-payment consent process remain separate and unavailable here.

The Working Man's 12-Week Strength Blueprint: the first 3 paying clients may choose $199/month, kept as long as they stay enrolled, or $570 paid in full for 12 weeks. After the first 3 paying clients, the rate is $297/month, or $850 paid in full for 12 weeks. There is no long commitment. Founding clients keep $199/month as long as they stay enrolled; if you cancel and rejoin later, the current rate applies. After week 12, you can continue month to month at your rate. Cancel before the next renewal to prevent that charge; coaching continues through the paid period. Statutory rights are preserved.

30-day guarantee: Do your weekly check-ins and follow at least 80% of the plan for your first 30 days. If it's still not a fit, you choose: a refund of your most recent payment, or 30 more days of coaching free. If you paid in full, the refund is about $190 (one month's share), and I'll tell you the exact amount before you enroll.

The $199/month founding option starts with the application. Ryan confirms your fit and rate before enrollment. The $570 founding pay-in-full price is listed on the site. Pay-in-full link is sent after your fit consultation. An application or consultation is not payment authorization. For questions, email ryangrandafit@gmail.com.
