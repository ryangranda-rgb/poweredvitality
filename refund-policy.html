Payment and cancellation information

Updated November 4, 2026

No payment or paid enrollment can be completed through the public application. The final paid-coaching agreement and recurring-payment consent process remain separate and unavailable here.

The Working Man's 12-Week Strength Blueprint offers monthly coaching or a 12-week pay-in-full block. Coaching options and pricing are discussed after your consultation, before any agreement or payment. Founding pricing is available to the first 3 qualifying paying clients; Ryan confirms eligibility and your exact rate before enrollment. Monthly coaching has no minimum commitment. You can cancel any time before the next charge, with no notice period. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current paid period. You can cancel online through the Stripe customer portal. Your portal link is provided at enrollment. You can also cancel by email or iMessage to Ryan. Founding monthly clients keep their agreed rate as long as they stay continuously enrolled; if you cancel and rejoin later, the current rate applies. After a 12-week pay-in-full block, there is no automatic renewal. You can opt in to continue month to month at your rate. Statutory rights are preserved.

30-day guarantee: Complete every check-in and follow at least 80% of your assigned workouts and 80% of your nutrition days. A nutrition day counts when it's logged, protein is hit, and calories are within 10% of target. If it's still not a fit, you choose either a refund or 30 extra days of coaching free. Monthly clients are refunded their latest payment. Pay-in-full clients are refunded exactly one-third of what they paid. If you choose the refund, your coaching access continues to the end of your current paid month (for pay-in-full, the end of your current 4-week period). Claim by iMessage or email between day 30 and day 37. Claims after day 37 are declined unless Ryan waives the deadline. I review each claim personally. Approved refunds go out within 7 days.

Start with the application. Ryan confirms your fit, coaching options and rate after your consultation, before enrollment. The final pay-in-full price and payment link are shared after your fit consultation, before any agreement or payment. An application or consultation is not payment authorization. For questions, email ryangrandafit@gmail.com.
