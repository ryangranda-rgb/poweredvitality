A FEW QUICK ANSWERS

Before you
say hello.

Who is this coaching for?

I work primarily with men in their 20s and 30s, especially Christian men, who want to improve their physique, strength, confidence and consistency. Coaching is open to all adults, regardless of gender or beliefs. Your plan fits your experience, equipment and schedule.

What does the coaching include?

Personalized training through Everfit, practical nutrition and recovery habit guidance, weekly check-ins, messaging support Monday–Saturday and one monthly coaching call. I review progress with you and adjust your plan as needed.

What does it cost, and how do I start?

The first 3 paying clients receive the founding rate of $199/month, billed automatically and retained while continuously enrolled. Subsequent new clients pay $249/month. There is no minimum commitment. After cancellation, rejoining uses the then-current disclosed price.

We begin with your application, my personal review and a fit consultation. Coaching starts after the required readiness review, accepted agreement, prepared plan, agreed start date, separate recurring-billing consent and verified payment. Final paid-enrollment checks are still being completed. Messaging, requesting a call or applying does not enroll or charge you.

Once enrolled, cancel before your next renewal to prevent the next charge; access continues through your paid period. Statutory rights are preserved. See the payment and cancellation information.
