Before you start

A few things
to know.

Do I need to be fit already?

No. This coaching is designed to meet you at your starting point, including if you're new to training or returning after a break. We'll discuss your experience and what a manageable start looks like.

Will my founding rate increase after 3 months?

No. The 3 months refer to our initial founding enrollment window. Clients who join during that window are grandfathered at $199/month. After it closes, new clients pay $249/month for the same coaching service.

Is there a 3-month commitment?

No. The founding enrollment window is separate from your subscription. Coaching is billed monthly, and you can request cancellation at any time by emailing ryangrandafit@gmail.com. Requests are processed within 2 business days, with access continuing through your paid period. Review the full cancellation and refund policy before enrolling.

How does faith fit into the coaching?

My Christian faith shapes how I coach: with honesty, care, accountability, and grace. We'll connect your habits to what matters to you and build a routine that supports the life you want to live.

What results can I expect?

We'll set goals based on your starting point and track relevant progress, such as consistency, strength, body measurements, and confidence. Results and timelines vary. I don't promise a specific amount of weight loss or muscle gain.
