Before you start

A few things
to know.

Do I need to be fit already?

No. This coaching is designed to meet you at your starting point, including if you're new to training or returning after a break. We'll discuss your experience and what a manageable start looks like.

Will my founding rate increase after 3 months?

Founders keep $199/month while continuously enrolled. New enrollments are $249/month from January 5, 2027. After your subscription ends, rejoining uses the current disclosed price.

Is there a 3-month commitment?

No. There is no minimum commitment. Cancel before your next renewal to prevent that charge; coaching continues through your paid period. Use the billing portal or email ryangrandafit@gmail.com. A request received before renewal prevents the next charge. Statutory rights are preserved. The full policy and final agreement await California attorney review before this flow opens.

How does faith fit into the coaching?

My Christian faith shapes how I coach: with honesty, care, accountability, and grace. Jesus-centered encouragement is optional, and all beliefs are welcome. We'll connect your habits to what matters to you and build a routine that supports the life you want to live.

What results can I expect?

We'll set goals based on your starting point and track relevant progress, such as consistency, strength, body measurements, and confidence. Results and timelines vary. I don't promise a specific amount of weight loss or muscle gain.
