A FEW QUICK ANSWERS

Before you
say hello.

Do I need to be experienced in the gym?

No. You can be starting fresh, returning after a break or already training. We build around your current experience, equipment and realistic week. Gym and home training are both options.

What does the coaching include?

A personal training plan in Everfit, weekly check-ins, a monthly coaching call, video form feedback, program adjustments, and general nutrition and recovery-habit guidance. Message me anytime; I personally review and respond Monday–Saturday.

Do I need to be Christian?

No. Powered Vitality is built with Christian men in mind, and my faith informs the care and integrity I bring to coaching. Faith can come into conversation naturally if you want it to; there is no required spiritual curriculum, and all beliefs are welcome.

What does natural progress mean here?

The focus is consistent training, practical nutrition and recovery. I do not coach the use of performance-enhancing drugs or promise a particular physique, timeline or spiritual outcome. Progress varies from person to person.

What does it cost, and how do I start?

The founding rate is $199/month, retained while continuously enrolled. There is no minimum commitment. After cancellation, rejoining uses the then-current disclosed price.

We begin with a conversation and fit consultation. Coaching starts after the required readiness review, accepted agreement, prepared plan, agreed start date, separate recurring-billing consent and verified payment. Final paid-enrollment checks are still being completed. Messaging, requesting a call or applying does not enroll or charge you.
