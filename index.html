Is this for beginners or people restarting?

Yes. We build around your current experience and a realistic training week. Gym and home training are both options.

What does the coaching include?

A personal plan in Everfit, weekly check-ins, video form feedback, program adjustments and general nutrition and habit guidance. Message me anytime; I personally review and respond Monday–Saturday.

The founding rate is $199/month, retained while continuously enrolled. There is no minimum commitment. After cancellation, rejoining uses the then-current disclosed price.
