1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, information collected automatically when you use our Services, and information from third-party platforms used to deliver our Services.

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you enroll in our Services, complete our intake forms, communicate with us, or otherwise interact with us, we may collect the following categories of personal information:

  • Identity information: Full name, date of birth, age;
  • Contact information: Email address, phone number, mailing address;

  • Health and fitness information: Current height, weight, body composition goals, training history, fitness experience level, current injuries or physical limitations, prior surgeries, current medications, diagnosed medical conditions, PAR-Q responses, average sleep duration, self-reported stress levels, and other health-related information disclosed in your intake form;
  • Program data: Workout logs, nutrition logs, check-in responses, progress photos (if voluntarily submitted), and other data you provide through the Trainerize platform or our check-in forms;
  • Communications: Messages, emails, and other communications you send to us.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our Site, we and our third-party service providers may automatically collect certain information about your device and usage, including:

  • IP address and approximate geographic location;
  • Browser type and version;
  • Device type and operating system;
  • Pages visited, time spent on pages, and referring URLs;
  • Clickstream data and interaction data;
  • Cookie identifiers and similar tracking technologies.

1.3 Information From Third-Party Platforms

We use third-party platforms to deliver our Services. When you create accounts on these platforms, those platforms may collect additional information subject to their own privacy policies. These platforms include:

  • Stripe — payment processing;
  • Trainerize — program delivery and workout/nutrition tracking;
  • Skool — community platform;
  • Kit (ConvertKit) — email marketing and communications;
  • Jotform — intake forms and onboarding;
  • Make.com — workflow automation;
  • DocuSign — electronic signatures.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the personal information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide and deliver the Services: Building and delivering your custom training program and nutrition plan, processing your payments, managing your subscription, responding to your check-ins, and communicating with you about your program;
  • To personalize your experience: Tailoring coaching plans, recommendations, and communications based on your goals, health history, and program data;
  • To ensure your safety: Reviewing PAR-Q and health history information to identify any contraindications before delivering programming;
  • To communicate with you: Sending program updates, check-in reminders, accountability messages, billing notifications, and responses to your inquiries;
  • To send marketing communications: Sending promotional emails, newsletters, and special offers (you may opt out at any time);
  • To send SMS messages: Sending account alerts, check-in reminders, and coaching communications via text message (you may opt out by replying STOP);
  • To improve our Services: Analyzing usage patterns, program outcomes, and client feedback to improve our coaching programs and platform;
  • To comply with legal obligations: Meeting applicable legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations;
  • To protect our rights: Enforcing our Terms and Conditions, resolving disputes, and preventing fraud or unauthorized use of our Services.

Our Services require us to collect sensitive health and fitness information, including information about medical conditions, injuries, medications, and physical limitations.
