What this notice covers

This notice explains how Powered Vitality handles information for adult online coaching in the United States, including consultation, enrollment, private readiness review, payment administration and ongoing coaching. Ryan Granda manages the coaching relationship. The public application has its own notice at https://poweredvitality.netlify.app/application-privacy. Applying does not enroll you or authorize payment.

Information you provide

When you apply, we receive your name, email, adult confirmation, optional Instagram handle and selected answers about nonmedical fitness goals, experience, schedule, equipment, general habits and interest in coaching. The application includes an administrative reference and form version. Please do not put diagnoses, injuries, medications, health documents or photos in that application.

During consultation and private intake, we ask for information needed to understand your goals, available training time, equipment, experience, ordinary food preferences, support preferences and readiness to participate. Readiness information may include limitations relevant to exercise, screening completion and follow-up needs. Provide only what is relevant; do not send complete medical records or identification documents unless a specific need and an appropriate private method have been explained. Coaching is not medical diagnosis, treatment or an emergency service.

During coaching, we receive the messages, workout activity, task responses and progress information you choose to provide through the coaching platform. Agreed measurements may include body weight, body measurements and training loads. Photos and testimonials are optional and need separate permission for any public or promotional use. Optional faith encouragement does not require you to state your religion.

For enrollment, we retain the agreement, identified version of the terms, signature and separate recurring-payment consent, related timestamps and delivery records. Signing services may also record authentication and technical transaction information, including IP address, to document the transaction.

For billing, Stripe collects payment details through its hosted checkout and customer portal. We use customer and subscription references, payment and invoice status, and cancellation or refund records to administer your service. Do not send card numbers, security codes, passwords or bank credentials to Ryan by email, application or coaching message.

How we use information

We use information to review fit, arrange your consultation, assess whether further readiness review is needed, prepare and adjust your plan, communicate with you, document your choices and agreement, administer payment and cancellation, respond to privacy requests, protect accounts and keep records required for legitimate business and legal purposes. An application is not marketing or SMS consent. Optional permission to use a photo or testimonial is separate from permission to coach you.

Where information goes

  • Netlify hosts the website and stores submitted public applications. Application notifications and correspondence are handled through Google/Gmail using ryangrandafit@gmail.com.
  • Google Drive stores completed agreements and necessary intake records in separate private business folders under ryangrandafit@gmail.com. Ryan is the only owner or collaborator currently granted access to those archive folders. Client copies are provided separately; archive folders are not shared publicly.
  • Google Forms collects private intake through access granted to your verified Google identity. Ryan checks your access before collecting your answers. The form collects your verified email, does not display response summaries to respondents, and has no linked response spreadsheet. Intake access is separate from the public application and does not give you access to the business archive or other clients’ records.
  • Zoom provides the online consultation and processes the audio, video, display name and any chat you choose to share. Our policy is to keep consultations unrecorded by default. A recording or another new capture of the conversation would require a separate explanation and any permission required before it begins. Ryan may keep necessary written coaching notes.
  • Docusign handles delivery, signature, consent fields and the completed agreement and transaction certificate. Google/Gmail handles delivery of your enrollment copy, billing confirmations and your reply providing separate recurring-payment consent, together with related correspondence. Private readiness answers do not belong in the signature packet.
  • Stripe handles hosted payment, recurring subscriptions and the customer portal. It maintains its own payment and transaction records.
  • Everfit provides your individual coaching account, workouts, tasks, messages and progress records. Ryan sends an individual invitation after the enrollment and payment steps are complete.
  • Notion holds a minimal administrative client record: contact/reference details, agreement and consent status, readiness status, agreed start date, and Stripe and Everfit references. Detailed readiness answers are not copied into that roster.

Ryan reviews information for coaching and administration. The named technology providers also process information to deliver their services and may involve their own authorized personnel or providers. Restricted access does not mean no provider can process the information. We may disclose necessary information when legally required. Any other intended recipient or new use that needs your permission will be explained before that disclosure.

These services have their own privacy notices and account controls. Their notices describe their own activities and do not replace Powered Vitality's responsibilities. Depending on the provider, processing and storage may occur outside your state or country. We do not promise a particular storage region or that these services are suitable for every kind of medical information.

Website storage and tracking

The public application code uses sessionStorage in the current browser tab for noncontact selections and a sending reference. It does not write your name, email, Instagram handle or adult confirmation to that storage. Your browser may independently autofill or restore values. “Clear unsent answers” clears the local draft; it does not delete an application already submitted to Netlify or email. Successful submission clears the saved choices and sending reference.

The application code does not include third-party advertising or analytics trackers and does not change its behavior in response to a browser Do Not Track signal. We do not use that code to track your activity across third-party websites. Hosting and form providers still process technical request information to operate their services. When you follow a link to or use Google, Zoom, Docusign, Stripe, Everfit or another provider’s service, that provider may collect technical information and use cookies or other technologies under its own privacy notice. Some providers and their partners may collect activity over time and across different websites. This notice does not promise that third parties never track activity. Applicable privacy choices and rights remain available.

How long information is kept

We keep each record for as long as it is needed for its specific purpose or an applicable retention requirement. Ryan reviews retention manually. In deciding what remains necessary, he considers whether an application or coaching relationship is still active, whether a request or dispute is unresolved, the sensitivity of the information, whether a smaller record would meet the need, and applicable legal, tax, accounting and insurance requirements.

Application and scheduling records support fit review and related follow-up. When that process ends, Ryan reviews whether any information is still needed. Readiness and coaching records support safe plan delivery, adjustments and necessary service history; when coaching ends, Ryan reviews the records and keeps only what remains needed for applicable recordkeeping or unresolved matters. Billing records support payments, cancellation, refunds, accounting and tax obligations. Optional photos and testimonials are kept only for an approved purpose and any necessary record of that permission.

Verification of affirmative recurring-payment consent is retained for at least three years from consent and at least one year after the contract ends, using whichever date is later. That minimum applies to consent verification; it does not set a universal period for every coaching or health-related record. Records subject to a legal preservation obligation may be kept longer.

When information is no longer needed and no retention requirement or hold applies, Ryan arranges its deletion from the business systems and copies under his control. Deletion is a manual process. Closing coaching access or clearing an unsent application does not automatically delete archived records, all backups or a provider’s independent records. Providers may retain information under their own service, security and legal requirements.

Your questions and choices

Email ryangrandafit@gmail.com to ask what information Ryan holds or to request access, correction or deletion. Please identify the email you used for coaching and the request; do not include passwords, card details or a copy of your ID in the initial message. Ryan may need to confirm your identity before disclosing or changing private information. Some information must be retained for legal, accounting, security or other applicable reasons. If a request cannot be completed in full, we will explain the applicable limitation. Rights and response requirements depend on the laws that apply to your circumstances.

You can leave optional questions unanswered and decline optional photos, testimonials or faith encouragement. If essential information is unavailable, we may need to discuss whether coaching can proceed safely. Requesting deletion and canceling a subscription are different actions. To stop future charges, use your cancellation method or tell Ryan you want to cancel; a privacy request will not be treated as permission to keep charging after a clear cancellation request.

Adults and security

Our application and enrollment workflow is for adults age 18 or older. If information about a child has been submitted, contact Ryan so it can be addressed appropriately. We use access restrictions and the providers' account controls, but no transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Do not use the public application or ordinary email for detailed health histories.

Changes to this notice

We will post updates with a new effective date and provide additional notice or obtain permission when required. A new notice does not turn an optional photo, testimonial, recording or marketing use into an approved use.
