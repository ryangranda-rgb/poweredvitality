Powered Vitality

Application privacy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

Powered Vitality LLC operates this application. Application contact: Union City, California. Email: ryangrandafit@gmail.com.

This notice covers the public coaching application and application-related email. Paid coaching, health intake, signatures and payment enrollment require a separate finalized process and are not available through this application.

What the application asks

The application asks for your name, email, optional Instagram handle, confirmation that you are 18 or older, and choices about your training experience, nonmedical goal, general activity routine, realistic training days, schedule, equipment, consistency obstacles, general eating-habit support, reason for seeking coaching and comfort with the disclosed monthly offer.

It also includes a form version and application ID for administration, an email-notification subject and a blank spam-check field. There are no uploads or open-ended health questions. Do not send diagnoses, injury details, medications, documents, photos or other sensitive health information.

How answers are handled

Netlify hosts this website and stores submitted live applications through Netlify Forms. Ryan reviews applications through his authenticated Netlify account. The website does not display a public list of application answers.

Application email is handled through Google/Gmail at ryangrandafit@gmail.com. This includes owner application notifications and Ryan's manual application-related replies. Ryan uses the answers to review coaching fit and, if appropriate, arrange a 30-minute Zoom consultation personally by email. Ryan offers times, confirms your chosen date, time and time zone, then sends the Zoom invitation.

Netlify handles technical request information in providing hosting and form processing. This application uses system fonts; it does not request fonts from Google. The application itself has no configured advertising or analytics tools and does not change its behavior in response to a browser Do Not Track signal. This does not prevent hosting or email providers from handling technical information for their services. The providers describe their handling in their own privacy notices: Netlify privacy notice and Google privacy policy.

What applying does

Applying asks Ryan to review fit and contact you about your application. It does not enroll you, authorize payment, complete health intake, sign a coaching agreement, waive claims or consent to marketing or SMS. Acceptance is never automatic. A separate finalized agreement is required before coaching.

Browser storage

The application code keeps selected noncontact choices in sessionStorage for the current browser tab so you can move between steps. It does not write your name, email, Instagram handle or adult confirmation to that storage. Those fields are kept in memory while completing the form. The application also keeps an application reference in this tab when sending, so an uncertain receipt can be checked without automatically posting again. Selecting Clear unsent answers removes the local draft and reference; it does not delete a submitted application from Netlify or email. Successfully finishing removes saved choices and the local reference. Your browser may separately provide autofill or restore form values.

Questions and requests

To ask about information Ryan holds, or request correction or deletion, email ryangrandafit@gmail.com. Application retention is handled manually for application follow-up and recordkeeping. Ryan handles requests to review, correct or delete information manually, subject to any required retention.

Updates to this notice

Updates are posted on this page with a revised effective date. Additional notices will be provided when legally required.
